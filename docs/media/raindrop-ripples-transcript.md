# Raindrop-ripple demonstration description

The silent two-second, 640 by 360, 60-frames-per-second video has four labelled
synthetic panels. Across the top, blue striped artwork is refracted by the
`rings`, `droplets` and `storm` variants. The clean ring travels out from the
centre; the droplet lenses gently pulse at several stable seeded positions; the
storm combines several overlapping travelling ripples. The lower full-width
panel shows the blue outgoing artwork washing into green artwork through the
`storm` field. Refraction strengthens around the moving reveal, peaks during the
middle of the transition and resolves to the undistorted green target at the end.

The video has no audio, captions or private campaign material. It demonstrates
software output only; it does not establish physical rainfall accuracy, musical
fit, GPU parity or artistic approval.
