# Original authored score fixture

These two original C-major bars are demonstration material authored for
FrameRudiments, rather than a transcription or quotation of a song.
Actual raster pages were exported with `integration/export_note_score.mjs`,
VexFlow 5.11.0 and its bundled Bravura font. Glyph rectangles and literal authored
spellings are retained in layout.json. PNG hashes are verified by the native
qualifier before binding each occurrence to the admitted note-timeline SHA-256.

VexFlow: MIT, https://github.com/vexflow/vexflow/blob/master/LICENSE
Bravura: SIL Open Font License 1.1,
https://github.com/steinbergmedia/bravura/blob/master/LICENSE.txt
The renderer consumes raster pages; no font binary or VexFlow runtime is
redistributed in the Python wheel. The library licence is retained below.
