# Recorded flame source

The atlas is a transformed excerpt of the Philipps-Universitaet Marburg
graphics/multimedia studio's gas-burner recording, released under CC0:
https://www.uni-marburg.de/en/fb12/research-groups/grafikmultimedia/research/studio

Original download:
https://www.mathematik.uni-marburg.de/~thormae/misc/fire_720p_120hz_from_right_short.mp4

The source page/filename describes 720p/120Hz, but the downloaded stream
actually probes as 1920x1080 at 60000/1001 fps. The included atlas is explicitly
resampled to 60 fps. Crop, rotation, key and original SHA-256 are recorded in
atlas.json. Original audio is discarded. A warm colour grade is optional
and is recorded separately by FireRenderer; it is not a temperature measurement.

This CC0 asset is separate from FrameRudiments' MIT software licence.
